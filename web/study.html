The distillation study

A research-paper-style study: many ablations, one factor at a time, and an honest ranking of what moved the needle. Hypothesis to test: a ~1M-parameter student distilled from several foundation models, after a light fine-tuning on your own history, matches the large models' out-of-the-box accuracy on your data.

How to read the results. Each arm is one training recipe. Scores are relative WQL vs seasonal naive (lower is better). The key column is the paired ratio vs the reference arm: both are scored on the same windows, so window difficulty cancels out. Below 1 = better than the reference; an effect counts only if its 95% interval excludes 1. Background: Learn 2 · error bars, Learn 4 · distillation.

What we found

Each card is one controlled comparison: two recipes that differ in a single factor, scored on the same windows. The bar shows the ratio of their errors (left of the line = the first recipe is better) with its 95% interval.
