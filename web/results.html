Results so far

Every number here is read from results/*.json, produced by committed scripts and configs. Scores are relative WQL: each window's weighted quantile loss divided by seasonal naive's, aggregated with a geometric mean (lower is better; 0.5 = half the error of "repeat the last season"). Brackets and whiskers are 95% bootstrap intervals, resampling whole series.

No distillation results yet. The only student here is the preview (ground truth only, 3 minutes, one seed), and it was trained on these 13 series' history while the foundation models forecast zero-shot. It is not a fair comparison yet.
