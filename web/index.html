How it works, in four steps
  1. The pen tip is "now". Everything left of it becomes up to 512 numbers, one per time step. Shorter drawings are padded with "missing" values.
  2. Units do not matter. The model rescales your drawing itself (median and mean absolute deviation, then asinh), so a doodle and a power grid look alike to it.
  3. A small transformer (4 layers) reads 32 chunks of 16 steps and outputs 13 quantiles per future step: a median plus 12 positive gaps, so the bands can never cross.
  4. Backtest by hovering: the forecast restarts from the hovered point using only the past, and what you drew afterwards (dashed) is the truth it is judged against.

Training: the large teacher labelled 300k windows (synthetic + real data from many domains) once; the student learned to reproduce those labels. Learn how distillation works →
