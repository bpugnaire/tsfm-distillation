Draw a time series, get a probabilistic forecast

Draw left to right in the grey area. A ~1M-parameter student network forecasts the next 64 steps in your browser (onnxruntime-web, WASM), with quantile bands: 40% (dark), 80%, 98% (light).

This is the preview student: trained on ground truth only for ~3 minutes, one seed. It exists to build and test the export + browser pipeline. The distilled student comes later.

What happens when you lift the pen

  1. Your stroke becomes 512 numbers (one per x pixel column of the context area). Columns you did not draw are NaN: the model treats them as missing.
  2. The ONNX graph normalises them itself: location = median, scale = mean absolute deviation, then asinh. So the units of your drawing do not matter.
  3. A 4-layer transformer reads 32 patches of 16 steps and predicts a median plus 12 positive gaps, so the 13 quantiles can never cross.
  4. The graph un-normalises, and you get quantiles in your drawing's units. Latency above is measured around session.run only.
